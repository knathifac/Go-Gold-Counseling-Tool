## Army source

`ARN44634-TC_7-22_7-000-WEB-2_1.pdf`, TC 7-22.7, **14 August 2025**, printed pages 31–36. The rubric uses 2-110–2-134 only.

Counseling/coaching/mentoring are 2-110–2-116. Officer–NCO relationships are 2-117–2-134. Those latter paragraphs do not define OARS. Role-specific expectations are conditional, and ordinary cadet mentorship does not establish an actual officer–NCO relationship. Doctrine describes voluntary mentorship beyond the superior/subordinate relationship; the local memo describes assigned cadet mentorship. They remain separate descriptions, not interchangeable mandates.
